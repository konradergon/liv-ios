Phase 4 mockup — the day hour grid

The month grid stays (it is the navigator). The day panel below it becomes Apple Calendar's shape: an all-day band, then a scrollable hour grid with timed items as positioned blocks and a red now-line on today.

Tap an empty hour → an event is created at that hour and opens as a desk tab for naming (the house "capture asks nothing" door, same as today's long-press). Drag a block → its time moves in 15-minute steps, committed on release; the duration is preserved.

Date-only tasks ride the all-day band keeping their status ring, so checking one off never needs the grid. Timeless events are pills, exactly as now.
