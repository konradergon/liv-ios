The floor

Today a note is somewhere you are, and a task is somewhere you visit. This puts one view always under you — Today, Calendar, Tasks, Find — and lets notes lie over it. Nothing here is built. It is drawn so you can look at it and say yes, no, or not like that.

1 · What it looks like now

The desk holds notes. Everything else is behind the door at the top left.

2 · The floor

One view is always under you. You never go to the calendar — you are on it, or you are standing on something else. The bar names the four floors and how many things you have open.

3 · Things you open lie over the floor

A note is still a tab. It covers the floor while you write, and closing it puts you back on the ground rather than on an empty desk.

Make a task while reading a note

Press the key, Task, type the name. The card is over your note; swipe it down and you are in the same sentence. No travel at all.

Check what is due today

Tap Today. One tap from anywhere, opening on the day you left it, scrolled where you left it.

Work a project

Pick the lens at the top centre. All four floors narrow to it: Today its due things, Calendar its dates, Tasks its tasks, Find its notes.

What this deletes

  • The library — not shrunk, deleted. Once the views are the ground it is a list of doors to rooms you are already in.
  • The empty desk and its No tabs hint. Close your last note and you land on Today.
  • The minimised card pill: a second tab plane, one item deep.
  • The back and forward arrows, which are the most Obsidian thing in the app.
  • Everything as a separate view — it becomes Find with an empty field.

4 · What needs your word

Each of these touches a ruling you made earlier, so none of it moves until you answer.

01

The one key costs a tap on Today and Tasks. Take it?

Recommended: yes. Today the button makes a task in one press; with four verbs it is press, Task, type. One key that behaves the same everywhere is worth one tap on two screens, and it buys a create door that works from inside a note.

02

Should Tasks list anything with a status, not just tasks?

Recommended: yes. A note you marked "doing" is work in progress. The calendar has always worked this way — it lists anything with a date — and nobody has complained.

03

Where do saved filters live once the library is gone?

Recommended: under the workspace name at the top centre. That button is the one thing on screen that says what you are looking at, which is exactly what a filter changes. It reverses where you put them on 11 August, so it needs your word.
